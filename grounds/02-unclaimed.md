# Requirements declared to rest on nothing

`U-NNN` — a requirement that rests on no hypothesis, said out loud, with the reason.
The reason is the statement of the record, and a declaration without one is an error.

Nothing here is a defect.
A requirement standing on nothing is the most useful reading this register produces, and no rule may ever demand that a requirement be claimed — where a link is mandatory it gets invented, and an invented link looks like knowledge.

*None at the moment.*
