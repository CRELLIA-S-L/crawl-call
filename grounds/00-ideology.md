# Ideology

`I-NNN` — what the system is for, in sentences somebody could disagree with.
One or a few; a project with a dozen has a list of features rather than an ideology.

An ideology is never measured.
It is what the hypotheses are measured *for*, and its `admissible_arguments` say what would count as a reason to revise it — which is the only thing standing between a considered change and whoever argues loudest.

### I-010 — Armed, talking prey is the game

```yaml
status: active
admissible_arguments: [a refuted H-010 or H-050 at release class, a playtest cohort of 20+ that describes the game without mentioning the bots' voices]
adopted: 2026-09-21
```

The game is worth making because a squad that realises it is being hunted by a snake — and says so — is something no other game gives; the hunt is the vehicle, the voices are the product.

**Rationale.** Concept §1 "what sells the game": not the bots' tactics but their voices. Everything else in the concept is in service of getting the player into situations where the squads talk.
